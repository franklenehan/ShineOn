About ShineOn

ShineOn is a personal companion to help track treatments, nutrition, research, and future plans throughout your cancer journey. It is designed to keep everything in one calm, organised place so you can focus on your wellbeing and the decisions that matter most.

Privacy

Your information is handled with care. Data related to your treatments, nutrition, and notes is stored securely on the ShineOn system and is only used to power the features you see in the app. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Please speak with your medical team before making any changes to your treatment or medications based on information recorded here.
